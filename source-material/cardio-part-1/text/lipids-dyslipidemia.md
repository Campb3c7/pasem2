## Slide 8

- Inflammation causes the area to become locally Prothrombotic

## Slide 15

- Free Fatty Acids/Triglycerides
- Free Fatty Acids (FFAs) are mobilized for tissues in 3 ways
  - Ingestion from Diet - chylomicrons
  - Adipose Cells release them – via albumin
  - Liver Produces them  - VLDL, IDL, LDL
- Tissues such as Muscle cells can absorb fatty acids for ATP production
- If not needed, stored in adipose cells

## Slide 16

- Lipoprotein Metabolism – Free Fatty Acids
- 1. Ingestion from Diet:
  - Consumed Fat is emulsified by Bile Acids during digestion
    - Bile Acids: synthesized by LDL cholesterol, and reabsorbed in the small intestine
  - Lipase is then able to break fat down into fatty acids
  - The small intestine cells are able to absorb FFAs
  - Within the cell: the FFAs are repackaged for transport into a Chylomicron Lipoprotein

## Slide 28

- CAD Prevention- Primary vs Secondary
- Primary prevention: management strategies provided prior to the onset of CVD related events. ​
  - This of course requires recognition! ​
  - But acting here provides the best long term results. ​
- Secondary prevention is a management plan for those who have already experienced CVD events: ​- The efforts to prevent progression and/or recurrence

## Slide 43

- Familial Hypercholesterolemia:
- 1. Familial Defective LDL Receptor:
- Homozygous FH, autosomal dominant, rare
  - Presents early in life
  - These patients develop CAD, aortic stenosis, and tendon xanthomas
  - Commonly can die of MI < 20 yo without early intervention
- Heterozygous FH, 1 in 500 individuals
  - Cells display ½ the normal amount of functional LDL receptors
  - CAC scores noted as early as 11 yo
  - Premature CAD (< 55 in men, < 65 in women), and clinical signs
- TX: Early Intervention of Lipid Management

## Slide 44

- Familial Hypercholesterolemia:
- 2. Familial Defective Apolipoprotein B
  - Autosomal dominant disorder
  - Defect in the Apo B protein results in impaired binding of LDL particles to LDL receptor
  - 1 in 750 Caucasians with HLD
  - Clinical Manefestations: Similar to Defective LDL Receptor, cholesterol deposit symptoms and Early Coronary Artery Dz
  - Treatment: Early Intervention of Lipid Management

## Slide 46

- Familial Hypertriglyceridemia

- Overproduction of hepatic VLDL, mutation/cause unknown

## Slide 47

- Familial Hypertriglyceridemia
- 2. Under Utilization
- Lipoprotein Lipase Deficiency
  - Mutation in the LPL gene resulting in deficiency of LPL synthesis/function
  - This leads to increased circulating Chylomicron and VLDL and severe hypertriglyceridemia
  - Tx:
    - Low fat diet is primary treatment (avoid creating chylomicrons)
    - Secondary factors should be addressed
    - VLDL/TG lowering medications ( fish oil/Vacepa)

## Slide 50

- Dyslipidemia Diagnostics
- LIPID PANEL
- Measured directly: Total Cholesterol, TG, HDL cholesterol
- Calculated: VLDL cholesterol, LDL cholesterol
  - IF TG < 400:
    - VLDL c = TG/5
    - Friedewald Equation: LDL c = Total Cholesterol – VLDL c – HDL c
  - IF TG > 400, VLDL c and LDL c unable to be determined

## Slide 51

- Dyslipidemia Diagnostics
- Example:
- Directly Measured:
  - Total Cholesterol = 200
  - Triglycerides = 150
  - HDL = 50
- What are the LDL cholesterol and VLDL cholesterol values?
  - VLDLc = 150/5 =30
  - LDLc = 200-30-50=120

## Slide 67

  - MOA: inhibits HMG CoA reductase enzyme involved in the cholesterol synthesis pathway

  - Decrease in All Cause Mortality with statin use
  - Limits progression and may even cause regression of atherosclerosis

## Slide 68

  - Pregnancy: Generally discontinue statins during pregnancy; avoid routine use.

## Slide 74

- Dyslipidemia Treatment 			Ezetimibe
- Cholesterol Absorption Inhibitors:
  - MOA: Inhibits absorption of cholesterol at the brush border of the small intestine via the sterol transporter, Niemann-Pick C1-Like1 (NPC1L1)

## Slide 75

- Dyslipidemia Treatment 			Ezetimibe
- Cholesterol Absorption Inhibitors:
- IMPROVE-IT study:​- Published in 2015, NEJM​- Multi-center, double blind, randomized controlled trial of 18,144 patients hospitalized for ACS/followed over 6 years​
- - Statin + Ezetimbe (Experiment Group)​    - Statin alone (Control Group)​​
- Among patients with recent ACS, adding ezetimibe to statin therapy provides a modest but statistically significant reduction in major cardiovascular events compared with statin therapy alone, without a demonstrated reduction in cardiovascular or all-cause mortality.

## Slide 79

- Dyslipidemia Treatment	Bile Acid Sequestrants
- Bile Acid Sequestrants:
  - MOA: Binds to bile acids which leads to increased fecal excretion of fats & cholesterol
  - ↓ LDL, ↑ HDL
  - SE: nausea, bloating, cramping, diarrhea or constipation, complications with absorption of other medications, inhibition of absorption of fat soluble vitamins, ↑ TGs

  - Medication of choice for dyslipidemia in pregnancy

## Slide 82

- Dyslipidemia Treatment			Fish Oil
- Omega-3 Fatty Acids: (Fish Oil, O3-FA, EPA, DHA)​
- MOA: Decrease hepatic VLDL production, lowering triglycerides.
- Lipid effects: Prescription omega-3 fatty acids (4 g/day) can reduce TG by approximately 20–45%.
- Medications:
- Icosapent ethyl (Vascepa): EPA-only, 2 g PO BID.
- Omega-3-acid ethyl esters (Lovaza): EPA + DHA, 2 g PO BID.
- SE: GI upset, increased risk of atrial fibrillation; possible increased bleeding risk.
- CV outcomes: Vascepa has demonstrated reduced major cardiovascular events in selected high-risk patients receiving statin therapy. This benefit has not been established for Lovaza or OTC fish oil supplements.

## Slide 87

- What is Metabolic Syndrome?
- Diagnosed if the patient has 3+ of the following:
- Waist circumference > 40 inches in men and > 35 inches in women
- Triglycerides  ≥ 150 milligrams per deciliter of blood (mg/dL)
- High-density lipoprotein cholesterol (HDL) < 40 mg/dL in men or < 50 mg/dL in women
- Fasting glucose ≥ 100 mg/dL
- BP values of systolic ≥ 130 mmHg and/or diastolic ≥ 85 mmHg
